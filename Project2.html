About this website

This project is a factbook style website about the New Zealand bird called kororā, also known as the little penguin. In my website, I explained how they grow, key facts about their behaviour, what they look like, and their habitats. The goal of this project is to help people learn basic information about kororā in a simple and friendly way.

I created this website as part of my Year 11 Digital Technology assignent. Through this project, I developed my skills in HTML, CSS, layout design, image styling, and organising information clearly for users.
